Eighteen ways to animate the comparison

Same story — vendor sprawl today versus one WorldPosta ecosystem — told with eighteen different animation mechanics. Options 8–10 keep the two cards and focus on an animated shadowed arrow curving between them; option 11 rebuilds the mountain-curve seam from your screenshot, animated. Scroll through, play with each one, and pick the option you want on the overview page. Everything uses the house tokens, so the winner drops straight into index.html.

Option 1

The Handover — drag between two worlds

One wide stage instead of two cards. The left world is today’s vendor sprawl; the right world is the WorldPosta ecosystem. A glowing divider sweeps slowly on its own — until the reader grabs it and switches the ecosystem on themselves. The most interactive option: the exec literally performs the handover.
